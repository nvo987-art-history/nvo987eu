# Changelog

All notable changes to NVO987.eu are documented in this file.

The format follows a simplified version of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Planned
- Continuous improvements to the Paris cultural events interface.
- Improvements to event-data validation and normalization.
- Additional machine-readable metadata and discovery resources.

## [1.0.0] - 2026

### Added
- NVO987.eu cultural events agenda.
- Paris Open Data integration through `events.json`.
- Event search.
- Configurable result limits.
- Responsive premium interface.
- Legal and source/licensing sections.
- Privacy-oriented configuration without cookies or tracking.
- `robots.txt`.
- `sitemap.xml`.
- RSS and Atom feeds.
- `llms.txt`.
- `ai.txt`.
- `security.txt`.
- `ads.txt`.
- `CITATION.cff`.
- `codemeta.json`.
- `foaf.xml`.
- `humans.txt`.

### Data
- Cultural event information is derived from public data made available through Paris Open Data / Ville de Paris.
- The official source remains authoritative when event information changes, is corrected, cancelled or removed.

### Privacy
- No cookies are used by the site.
- No analytics or behavioural tracking is intentionally implemented.
- No personal-data collection is intentionally implemented by the agenda interface.
